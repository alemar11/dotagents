# Explore Report

Return a concise answer in the current conversation, with detail proportionate
to the question. Lead with the finding or recommendation, then explain:

- the agreed scope and any unconfirmed assumptions;
- observed evidence and citations, distinguished from inferences;
- material contradictions, missing evidence, and their impact on confidence;
- the overall outcome from [states.md](states.md) and the next useful action.

When helpers contributed, summarize their distinct findings and any coverage
or independence limitation. Do not include a slot ledger, model telemetry, or
launch receipts in the report unless needed to explain a blocker or requested
by the user. Keep helper identities in the conversation for follow-up.

State `Changes made: None`. Recommendations do not authorize implementation
or saving a report.
