# Review Thread Resolution

GitStack owns reply and resolution requests, provider transport, receipts, and
readback. Journal reply and resolve as separate one-use run-state operations.

Resolve only the exact accepted inline finding after current committed repair
evidence. On ambiguous transport, reconcile the original operation and never
post or resolve again. Summary-only findings have no synthetic thread.

Resolution grants no merge, enqueue, deploy, Goal, task, or worktree authority.
